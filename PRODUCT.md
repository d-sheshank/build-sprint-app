# PRODUCT.md

## 1. The job
When I need to prepare for an interview, I want to revise my history and map the relevant storeis to the job description to generate 3 STAR stories and pitch it as a clear value add, so I can Impress interviewers  and get best ratings.

Other moments it happens:  When I don’t feel ready with my current stories, when I remember something which i forgot earlier to add to my fact bank, when reframing for a new role requires overall view, When i want to get motivated to answer questions with an interactive brainstormer invested in my success rather than write a finished answer.

Who, by situation : Interviewees who are underprepared for an interview within a week but with relevant content. 
Today they hire: a mock interview, an interview kickstarter kind of training, write it themselves or ask a friend to review. A head hunter to review with limited technical know how.


(Advanced, optional) What needs doing: [ generate a fact back, establish credibility, adopt answers depending upon interviewer levels ]
(Advanced, optional) How they want to feel: [ Confident, prepared, thorough ]
(Advanced, optional) How they want to look to others: [ proactive, credible, reliable, trustworthy, capable ]


## 2. The switch
What they'd fire: Dedicated coach who would review answer and give feedback. The bandwidth will be a problem. Or doing it by themselves, we could offer a free tier. 

The forces that matter :
Push, outside them :  Next interview is scheduled with less time and the user needs the preparation to be thorough.  getting paid less, losing on interview, too much unknown information and not understanding interviewer questions

What the product does about each: What is important for next interview -> Proposes few stories based on the fact bank and few alternative pitches from users background and companies strategic objective which fall in role’s responsibilities
The one worry onboarding must remove: Get a pitch which aligns with everyone and positions for success.

## 3. The core flow
Today (what they do now, before your product):
1. I need to prepare for an interview
2. I work out of memory, start listing important information relevant to role
3. I spend hours trying to rewrite my stories and match the relevant job requirements either on computer, phone or paper.
4. Trying to find actual proof points in the powerpoints or emails I previously had. 
5. Then I take a break. And go back to step 2. 
6. After 3 to 4 iterations of 2-3-4
7. I end up choosing a story/ though I feel is right to lead the sale with. 
8. But the information is scattered all around or I am already low on energy.
9. Most often than not, I end up winging 50% of the interview
10. Even after spending days and nights preparing for the interview, it seems overwhelming because of the time it takes.
11. There by burning after getting any job or after 3-6 interviews 
12. Bandwidth to interview for multiple companies is limited

With my product:
1. I need to prepare for an interview
2. I upload JD and get a set of possible STAR answers with placeholders. 
3. I collect unorganized notes of my past projects, my profile and all the information that I can get hands on. 
4. I visit the buddy app and upload my profile, past projects and any other information along with target company, job description and any other information related to company and role. 
5. Then interactively, i answer questions one after another with an option to generate STAR and pitch anytime during the interactions like following
	1. What type of interview is this - option 1, option 2, something else 
2. Why do you want this job
3. Do you want to refine your fact bank - will be followed as another user flow later.
4. How much time do you have to spend. 
5. What are you most uncomfortable / least prepared about .. 
6. Once the stories are generated and pitch is prepared, it is presented in a narration format with one story per page at a time. And scroll to next story. (with visuals can be in later versions)
7. Each story has a popup option to give more context and sources as an overlay.
8. They are able to tweak the answers and export it as needed. 
This is job done
 
2nd flow: 
Any time a new information comes about company or role or past projects
STAR stories are further refined iteratively with more information about company, role or my projects. 
3rd flow (parked): 
Fact bank refinement happens with one question at a time without overwhelming the user
Like, do you think these two projects are hierarchical. 
What was your role during this time or project
What was the important metric at this point in time
Then make it entertaining to revisit in any free time to improve about it and share the exports as needed. 

Things it takes to get the job done today: Not done to satisfaction · With my product: 4 steps

What must not happen:
Step [n]: going into discussion phase without any concrete output or actions 
Step [n]: Providing non-factual numbers, hallucinating facts about company

Other flows (stop when the job is covered): Stop when the pitch is proposed and only change if new information is presented. 

## 4. Onboarding
First value (the moment it first does the job for them): Stories are ready in structured manner and I align with the pitch
The smallest commitment we ask for: Job description and they get ideal winning stories for most asked question with placeholders like xx yy zz for numbers. 
The worry it removes (from section 2): Paying hourly for a non-technical glorified HR coach.



From opening the link to the first value:
1. They open app on phone and upload Job description. App removes: the effort and money needed to get first proposal options. 
2. They open app on phone and add personal profile. App updates STAR stories and pitch accordingly. 
3. Clear narration of stories and pitch  removes: confusion in communication
4. Refine existing STAR format answers as much as needed.

Login: After initial 3 STAR stories to store them with user account.
What we don't ask on day one: Payment
What we ask later, and when: Payment after uploading job description for 3rd interview

## 5. v1
Does (the must haves: one person finishes the one job): the JD going in, the round question, 3 stories with xx blanks, filling in their numbers, the source on every number, export.
Doesn't (not this sprint: parked, not forgotten): [ No market research, no voice search, no roadmap planning, no mock interview, no voce practie, no role matching, no fact-bank refinement and no hiring side flows]
Nice to have (only after the must haves work):  
Alignment beyond STAR stories answering why this role and why now. Motivation and commitment questions
Have a hierarchical view of the achivements or numbers like aggregated across projects - total saving, total features deployed, total years of work exp, highest deal value, maximum number of team members, worked in countries. etc
Have multiple layers of answers in the same STAR story with a slider based on the type and level of interview
How I'll know it worked (what they do again, not what they say): They upload another job description and review STAR answers for less than 3 iterations.
