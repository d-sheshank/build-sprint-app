# Story prep

A focused interview preparation workspace. The pasted role stays beside the story drafts on desktop; the page becomes a single column on phones. No navigation to future milestones.

Colors: page #f5f8fc, input workspace #e9eff9, ink #172d4c, action blue #234db5, supporting text #50627b, dividers #d7e0ed. DM Sans carries headings and body. A continuous reading column keeps each STAR story together, with small blue section labels and generous separation between stories.

States: empty guidance, disabled empty submit, busy generation, recoverable error, completed drafts. Drafts explicitly distinguish unknown facts in brackets from unknown numbers as xx. No personal data collection or saved history in this milestone.
